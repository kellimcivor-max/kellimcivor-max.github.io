      02 · Prompt library

      Prompts ready for engineering, product, UX, and leadership

      Start with these prompts, then save the ones that work as shared agents for each team.

      Engineering

      Configuration check

      For [machine/configuration] and symptom [issue], find the applicable parts, software versions, prior issues, and service information. Separate confirmed matches from possible ones. Don’t infer compatibility.

      Keeps machine variants and software versions straight.

      Engineering

      Defect trace

      Trace defect [ID] back to its requirements, related code changes, and test results. Show what changed recently, who owns each area, and which tests cover it.

      Shortens the time to the first useful lead.

      Engineering

      Test-failure triage

      For failing test [name], find similar past failures, their root causes, and the fixes applied. Rank the likely causes and cite the evidence behind each one.

      Helps PV&V teams reuse what’s already known.

      Product

      Change-impact review

      For this proposed change to [product or feature], find the relevant design decisions, technical dependencies, test results, defects, and customer feedback. Summarize impacts for engineering, product, and UX. Cite sources and call out gaps or conflicting evidence.

      One review, framed for every discipline.

      Product

      Decision history

      Why did we choose [approach] for [feature]? Find the original decision, the alternatives considered, and anything that has changed since. Link each point to its source.

      Recovers context that usually lives in people’s heads.

      UX

      Field-feedback themes

      Summarize evidence about [customer or field issue] across available service and product sources. Group it by configuration or use context, then list the implications for product, engineering, and user experience.

      Connects the field experience to design decisions.

      UX

      Workflow pain points

      For the [display or app] workflow [name], find known usability issues, related tickets, and research findings. Show which issues recur and which teams own the fixes.

      Turns scattered signals into a prioritized view.

      Leadership

      Technology opportunity brief

      Review [initiative or technology] and prepare a decision brief covering the customer problem, evidence, affected teams, dependencies, risks, and the smallest useful next test. Cite sources and list unanswered questions.

      Gives every bet a clear next test.

      Leadership

      Weekly enablement pulse

      Summarize this week’s progress, blockers, and decisions across [teams or initiatives]. Highlight what needs my attention and who owns each item.

      A one-page view of the work across teams.

      Leadership

      New-team-member ramp

      Create a ramp guide for a new engineer joining [team]: key systems, recent decisions, important documents, and the people to meet—with links to each.

      Helps new engineers become productive sooner.
