A day in the life

From raw signal to a governed, ready-to-act work product

The agent runs the reliability engineer's workflow continuously — not on a schedule, but the moment a signal crosses a threshold. Every recommendation is grounded in your own records, cited, and permissions-aware.

01

Sees the signal

Pulls maintenance history from the CMMS, prior work orders, condition-monitoring alarms, and OEM manuals — no dashboards to check.

02

Reasons across context

Correlates recurring failures, parts, warranty status, and past fixes across silos that don't normally talk to each other.

03

Flags the exception

Only what deviates: a failure signature repeating, capital sitting idle, a warranty about to lapse — ranked by cost and urgency.

04

Drafts the action

A diagnosis brief, the right expert to call, and a draft work order or claim — queued for review, governed by permissions.
