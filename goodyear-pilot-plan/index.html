01

Overview

The purpose of this pilot is to validate Glean's impact in a live Goodyear environment, with real users and real Goodyear data — and to show it delivers value your current Copilot deployment is not yet reaching.

●Connect trusted knowledge

Link people to permission-aware information across existing systems — including legacy and fragmented environments — without moving data out of source systems.

▲Prove unique value

Demonstrate GRC, role-based access, and pre-packaged intelligence across a CEO/KPI dashboard, engineering productivity, and manufacturing cost analysis.

✓Define the path to scale

Collect feedback, evaluate adoption function by function, document before-and-after workflows, and set the criteria for a broader enterprise rollout.

02

The Challenge We're Solving

Goodyear wants one impactful, pervasive AI experience — a single-screen, agentic platform that serves every role from the C-suite to the shop floor — while minimizing manual plumbing and duplicative spend.

⁂Siloed, antiquated systems

Inconsistent systems across business units slow decisions. Engineering knowledge and legacy environments (Team Center-style) make prior knowledge hard to reach.

$Copilot value gap

7,500 of 10,000 Copilot Premium licenses are assigned, but only ~20–30% of potential is realized — and the goal is fewer, more effective AI tools, not more.

☉Governance across distributed data

Stronger GRC, role-based access, permissions, and auditability are required across fragmented data, including SharePoint sprawl.

⊞One governed front door

A platform that can sit beneath existing front ends, including Copilot, rather than becoming another isolated point tool.

06

Proposed Starter Workflows

One horizontal assistant motion for all pilot users, plus focused operational, engineering, and manufacturing tracks.

☰Track 1 — Horizontal Assistant

  • Plan my day — prioritized brief from calendar, mail, and tasks
  • Weekly catch-up on what changed across my workspaces
  • Show my open action items across Teams, email, tickets, and docs
  • Prep me for my next meeting with related docs, threads, and owners
  • Find the latest version of a document and who last changed it

⚙Track 2 — IT Support / Operational Retrieval

  • Ticket Status Tracer — status, owner, and last comment in one answer
  • Known-Fix Finder — cited known fix from runbooks and Stack Overflow
  • Teams IT-support agent spanning SharePoint, support content, and collaboration systems

✎Track 3 — Engineering Productivity

  • Locate prior design and materials knowledge faster
  • Retrieve technical documentation and prior decisions across repositories
  • Glean as the retrieval layer behind PGR Copilot — better answers, no rebuild

🏭Track 4 — Manufacturing / Operations

  • Surface SOPs, troubleshooting steps, and cost knowledge faster
  • Role-based answers (e.g. finance user pulling SAP data for payables triage)
  • Structured + unstructured cost analysis via Snowflake + SharePoint

Success Criteria

Success is measured across technical performance and business outcomes, with track-level metrics agreed up front — and a clear hypothesis to avoid unfocused testing.

⚙Technical Success

  • Agreed Phase 1 sources connect successfully
  • Permissions and role-based access mirror source systems correctly
  • Glean answers include valid citations
  • Multi-source questions return grounded answers
  • No critical permission or hallucination failures in test scenarios

★Business Success

  • Pilot users report faster access to answers and information
  • At least one engineering or manufacturing workflow shows measurable improvement
  • Demonstrable unique value beyond current Copilot usage
  • Executive team completes a go / no-go decision on jointly agreed evidence
TrackExample Measure
Horizontal assistantUsers report at least 30 minutes saved per day on prep, catch-up, or retrieval
IT supportGlean returns ticket status, owner, last comment, and context correctly on a majority of agreed test cases
EngineeringEngineers confirm faster retrieval of prior knowledge and less duplicated work
Manufacturing / operationsOperations users confirm faster time-to-answer on recurring cost and troubleshooting questions
Executive dashboardingLeadership confirms cross-system KPI questions can be answered in one place with trusted sources
