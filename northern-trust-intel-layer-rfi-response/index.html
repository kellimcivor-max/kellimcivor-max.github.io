1.2 · Our Understanding

From connected agents to an enterprise intelligence layer.

Byron has matured from SharePoint-connected agents (1.0) to a parent-agent orchestration model in Copilot Studio (2.0). Byron 3.0 is the leap to an enterprise intelligence layer that orchestrates Copilot Studio agents, pro-code LangChain/LangGraph agents, and third-party AI — under context-aware routing, governance, and a marketplace of approved agents.

Byron 1.0 — Retrieval

Individual connected agents

SharePoint-scoped agents; users had to know which agent to query; static retrieval, minimal orchestration.

Byron 2.0 — Orchestration

Parent-agent routing

A single entry point determines intent, builds context, and routes to child agents (HR, corp ops, workplace) in Copilot Studio.

Byron 3.0 — Intelligence Layer

Enterprise orchestration

Governed routing across Copilot Studio, pro-code agents & third-party AI; registry, marketplace, metadata & content hygiene at scale.

Risk & Objection Handling

We've heard your concerns. Here's how we answer them.

"We don't want to rip out Microsoft, Copilot Studio, or LangChain."
You don't. Glean sits underneath as the shared foundation — your front ends and agents stay in place. Customers routinely keep their own UIs and use Glean as the backend platform.
"How portable is the context layer if we ever exit?"
Observability and context data are exportable, connectors are configuration-driven, and access runs through open standards (APIs, MCP). We'll cover portability and exit options in depth under NDA in technical diligence.
"How do you validate model routing and maintain output quality?"
Routing decisions are traceable and continuously evaluated against accuracy/relevance/reliability metrics; retrieval is grounded in your governed data before inference to prevent hallucination and bias.
"Will security satisfy bank-grade, regulated requirements?"
Secure-by-design, permission-aware, SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001 / ISO 42001 certified, with SIEM export and HITL checkpoints — built for systemically-important institutions.
"Can you support client-facing / embedded experiences?"
Yes — with the same permission-aware governance and access controls applied to internal use, plus standardized handoff and human escalation for sensitive wealth and portfolio contexts.
"Buy vs. build — why not build this ourselves?"
Glean gives you the plumbing — connectivity, permissions, context, routing, governance — as a maintained product, so your teams build differentiated agents instead of re-engineering foundation as regulations and downstream systems change.
