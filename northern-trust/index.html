The Journey · Five Phases

From foundation to the front office

Each phase stands on its own value while laying the groundwork for the next — no rip-and-replace, no waiting for the whole vision to prove one step.

01
Phase 01 · Foundation

Connect Byron — the internal enterprise data layer

What if Glean sat on top of Byron and unified Northern Trust's enterprise data — with every existing access control carried through, untouched?

  • Glean indexes and connects to Byron and surrounding systems, respecting source-level permissions on every result.
  • A single governed retrieval layer — the plumbing beneath your Microsoft and LangChain agents, not a competing platform.
  • Foundation for context, connectivity, and APIs before any commercial scale-up.
02
Phase 02 · Enablement

Engineering & IT enterprise intelligence, powered by Glean

What if your engineers and IT teams had an enterprise intelligence layer that answered from code, tickets, docs and runbooks — in one place?

  • Developer productivity: search across repos, wikis, and knowledge without context-switching.
  • ITSM & operational efficiency — resolve issues faster with grounded, cited answers.
  • A low-risk internal launchpad that proves value and trust before client-facing use.
03
Phase 03 · Advisor Productivity

Integration with Wealth Advisors

What if a wealth advisor could ask one question and instantly pull the right policy, product detail, and client context — fully grounded, fully cited?

  • Advisors spend less time hunting across systems and more time with clients.
  • Answers assembled from approved, permission-aware sources — no guessing, no exposure.
  • Delivered inside the tools advisors already use, from Teams to internal portals.
04
Phase 04 · Trust & Control

Regulatory, compliance & governance

What if AI adoption strengthened your compliance posture instead of complicating it — every answer traceable, auditable, and in-policy?

  • Enterprise-grade governance: access controls, audit trails, and data residency honored end-to-end.
  • Citations on every response give reviewers a clear line back to the source of record.
  • Governed guardrails let regulated workflows adopt AI with confidence, not caveats.
05
Phase 05 · The Vision

The knowledge layer behind the Private Client experience

What if Glean became the knowledge layer between your Private Client UI and the enterprise — the trusted brain your client experience quietly runs on?

  • Every client-facing surface draws grounded, governed answers from one intelligence layer.
  • A consistent source of truth spanning Byron, engineering, advisory, and compliance.
  • Northern Trust's differentiated client experience, powered by enterprise-wide knowledge.

Microsoft Copilot

Productivity inside M365

Excellent for individual productivity within the Microsoft suite, but bounded to the O365 ecosystem, dependent on OpenAI models, and reliant on third-party connectors — often without item-level permissions — for everything outside it. Glean is the horizontal layer that reaches the rest of Northern Trust's stack, including Byron.

Cloudflare AI

Edge & infrastructure AI

Powerful developer building blocks — Workers AI, AI Gateway, retrieval primitives — for teams assembling their own applications. It is infrastructure, not a governed, permission-aware knowledge layer for every employee. Glean delivers that layer out of the box.

Cowork (Anthropic)

Agentic assistants

Strong Claude-powered agents for knowledge workers, but agent-first rather than a shared enterprise index — a maturing connector set and single-model family. Glean grounds any agent (including Cowork or your LangChain stack) in one governed knowledge base.

Palantir Foundry

Complementary, not competitive

The factory for structured, high-value analytics — risk models, fraud detection — built for data scientists. It is complementary: Glean indexes Foundry's outputs so every employee can discover them in context, amplifying that investment rather than replacing it.

The Economics · Tokenomics

Glean reduces token cost — by making every AI interaction more efficient

As Northern Trust scales AI across the enterprise, token spend becomes the meter running on every query. Glean is engineered to keep that meter low — through better context, the right model, and reuse.

Context — reduce wasted tokens

Better context, fewer wasted tokens. Glean sends the model only what matters, so you stop paying to process noise on every call.

Models — route to the right model

The right model for each job. Glean auto-selects across 35+ LLMs, so you never overpay a frontier model for a task a smaller one handles.

Reuse — build context once

Build context once, use it everywhere. One enterprise context layer powers search, chat, agents, IDEs, and 3rd-party tools so teams never repay tokens to rediscover the same facts.
