Commercial structure
  • Models are priced at the vendor’s published rate, keeping the underlying model economics transparent.
  • Glean Flexible Model Management (FMM) is priced at 10% of total model consumption.
  • This structure gives Northern Trust freedom to evolve model strategy over time without changing the enterprise context and governance layer underneath.
Usage formula: Total Model Hub investment = vendor-published model consumption + 10% Flexible Model Management.
