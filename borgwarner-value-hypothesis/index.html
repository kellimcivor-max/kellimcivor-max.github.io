BorgWarner Value Hypothesis

A secure, connected AI foundation for BorgWarner

BorgWarner’s opportunity is to connect today’s AI investments, operational systems, and internal builder efforts into one trusted context layer — so teams can move faster, automate with confidence, and improve the quality of decisions across ITSM and operational workflows.

Current AI environment

BorgWarner already has meaningful AI momentum. The next step is making these tools work together with shared context, governance, and enterprise-wide usability.

Copilot

Broad productivity layer across Microsoft workflows, useful for everyday tasks but limited when enterprise context needs to span multiple systems.

Claude

Strong model capability for drafting and reasoning, best amplified when paired with secure access to company knowledge and business systems.

Open applications

Internal agent and app experimentation can move faster when builders do not need to recreate permissions, search, and connector logic from scratch.

Current state → Future state → Success measures

A practical view of how a connected AI foundation can move from fragmented tool usage to measurable operating value.

Current state

  • AI activity is spread across multiple tools and systems.
  • Useful answers often depend on context that lives in several places.
  • Internal builders can spend too much time recreating access, connectors, and workflow logic.

Future state

  • One secure, permissions-aware layer connects enterprise knowledge and applications.
  • Teams can search, reason, and automate across systems with more confidence.
  • Builders can focus on high-value workflows instead of integration plumbing.

Success measures

  • Reduced manual research and workflow time.
  • Faster time to launch or improve internal AI applications.
  • Higher trust in AI outputs for ITSM and operational use cases.
  • Smoother security and governance approval for broader rollout.
