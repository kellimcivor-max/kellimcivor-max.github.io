Microsoft Copilot (M365)

Productivity inside M365

Excellent for individual productivity within the Microsoft suite, but bounded to the O365 ecosystem, dependent on OpenAI models, and reliant on third-party connectors — often without item-level permissions — for everything outside it. Glean is the horizontal layer that reaches the rest of Key's stack, including Salesforce, Bloomberg, core banking, and your deal systems.

Google Gemini

Key's Google partnership

Powerful and native across Google Workspace, and central to Key's current pilots — but a single-model family, and token spend that can spike unpredictably as usage scales across the bank. Glean is model-agnostic: it routes to Gemini (or any of 35+ LLMs) for the right job and grounds every answer in one governed knowledge base, so you keep Gemini and cut the cost of running it.

In-House Build

Engineering-owned

Key's engineering teams already maintain Gemini, Copilot, and the API layer between them — deep expertise, but every new connector, permission model, and evaluation framework is another program to build and own. Glean is the productized version of that plumbing — permission-aware retrieval, 150+ connectors, and governance shipped as a platform — so your engineers orchestrate value instead of maintaining pipes.

The Economics · Tokenomics

Glean reduces token cost — by making every AI interaction more efficient

As Key scales AI across the bank, token spend becomes the meter running on every query — and it can run hot: a single weekend job can burn tens of thousands of dollars and exhaust a monthly allotment early. Glean is engineered to keep that meter low, through better context, the right model, and reuse — across Gemini and Copilot alike.

C

Context — reduce wasted tokens

Better context, fewer wasted tokens. Glean assembles what it can from your enterprise data before a query ever hits the LLM, so you stop paying to process noise on every call.

M

Models — route to the right model

The right model for each job. Glean auto-selects across 35+ LLMs — including Gemini — so you never overpay a frontier model for a task a smaller one handles.

R

Reuse — build context once

Build context once, use it everywhere. One enterprise context layer powers search, chat, agents, IDEs, and 3rd-party tools so teams never repay tokens to rediscover the same facts.

The Journey · Five Phases

From the deal desk to the front line

Each phase stands on its own value while laying the groundwork for the next — no rip-and-replace, no waiting for the whole vision to prove one step. This is the plan that starts where Key wins deals and grows to the enterprise.

01
Phase 01 · Foundation

Connect the enterprise data layer

What if Glean sat on top of Key's core systems and unified enterprise knowledge — firewalled, with every existing access control carried through, untouched?
  • Glean indexes and connects Salesforce, Bloomberg, Microsoft Teams, core banking, deal systems, and ServiceNow, respecting source-level permissions on every result.
  • A single governed retrieval layer that sits inside your enterprise — nothing goes out, nothing comes in — beneath your Gemini and Copilot ecosystem, not a competing platform.
  • Connectors and evaluation shipped as a product: no manual metadata layers, no per-source engineering program to maintain.
02
Phase 02 · The Wedge

Capital markets & coverage intelligence

What if a coverage banker came in every morning to one command center — pipeline, mandates, comps, and client signals surfaced automatically across Salesforce and Bloomberg — instead of reconciling terminals and tabs?
  • Automate deal origination and pipeline hygiene: Glean keeps Salesforce current, drafts RFP responses and journey maps, and builds role-specific daily dashboards in plain language.
  • Consolidate the systems bankers touch — Bloomberg, Salesforce, email, Teams, deal docs — into one view, so 30% of the day spent originating isn't lost to hunting for context.
  • A high-value, low-risk wedge in the business that cares about outcomes over infrastructure — proving trust before broader rollout.
03
Phase 03 · Frontline Productivity

Contact center, relationship & client service

What if a client-service agent or relationship banker could ask one question and instantly pull the right policy, product detail, and account context — fully grounded, fully cited?
  • Agents and relationship bankers spend less time hunting across systems and more time serving clients, with answers assembled from approved, permission-aware sources.
  • Every response respects who the user is — clients only surface data the banker is entitled to see, with no exposure of restricted holdings or records.
  • Delivered inside the tools teams already use, from Teams to servicing and contact-center consoles.
04
Phase 04 · Trust & Control

Risk, security, regulatory & compliance

What if AI adoption strengthened Key's security and compliance posture instead of complicating it — every answer traceable, auditable, and in-policy?
  • Enterprise-grade governance: access controls, audit trails, and data residency honored end-to-end — designed to meet one of the industry's most demanding security bars.
  • Citations on every response give reviewers and examiners a clear line back to the source of record — policy, procedure, or filing.
  • Governed guardrails let regulated, lending, and model-risk workflows adopt AI with confidence, aligned to your gen-AI governance council's standards.
05
Phase 05 · The Vision

The knowledge layer behind the client & banker experience

What if Glean became the knowledge layer between Key's client, banking, and capital-markets experiences and the enterprise — the trusted brain your teams quietly run on?
  • Every banker- and client-facing surface draws grounded, governed answers from one intelligence layer.
  • A consistent source of truth spanning capital markets, core banking, operations, and compliance.
  • Key's relationship-first reputation, powered by enterprise-wide knowledge at everyone's fingertips.
