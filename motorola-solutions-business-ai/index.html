04 · The Solution

Three foundations, one governed platform.

Glean delivers exactly what Business AI needs — connected context, orchestration without lock-in, and enterprise-grade governance — through three integrated pillars.

01

Enterprise Context

The Enterprise Graph + Personal Graph build a permission-aware understanding of Motorola's people, content, and systems — and how operations and business teams actually work. That grounding makes retrieval precise and answers trustworthy.

02

Glean Intelligence

Intelligent routing across ~30 models, a no-code agent builder, and continual learning. Retrieval runs against Glean's index before inference — cutting AI consumption cost 25–30% while improving answer quality.

03

Glean Protect

AI-powered access controls, native permission mirroring, full auditability, and agent guardrails. Every retrieval respects each user's identity — so Glean never surfaces what a user isn't authorized to see. Built for defense-grade governance.

09 · Addressing Your Concerns

We've heard the questions. Here's how we answer them.

"What does being an AI company mean outside the product?"

It means the business itself becomes AI-native. Glean is the layer that gets you there — one governed foundation where operations, supply, M&A, and strategy teams work through AI every day, not a product feature. That's the differentiation you build the ecosystem and partnerships around.

"We're already building agents in-house — why buy?"

A custom build solves one team's problem but carries ongoing maintenance and technical complexity, and its knowledge stays trapped. Glean turns that corpus into a governed enterprise asset — maintained as a product — so your team builds differentiated business agents instead of re-engineering foundation.

"We're a Google shop — will this duplicate our investment?"

It won't. Glean sits underneath as the shared context layer and reduces AI consumption cost ~25–30% by serving enterprise context before external LLM calls. Google Workspace and Gemini stay your productivity surface; Glean makes retrieval enterprise-wide across every system they can't reach.

"Will security and permissions hold for defense-adjacent data?"

Permission-aware by default: every retrieval respects each user's identity and entitlements, with native permission mirroring, full auditability, and Motorola-controlled encryption keys (CMEK/BYOK). Glean never surfaces what a user isn't authorized to see.

"How does this help a lean org — and how fast will teams adopt?"

Glean typically reaches 90%+ adoption with ~98% answer accuracy, and employees build their own agents in natural language — so a lean team does more without adding headcount. Connector maintenance shifts off your team, managed by Glean across 250+ sources.
