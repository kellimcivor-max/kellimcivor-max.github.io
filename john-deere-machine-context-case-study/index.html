02 · Executive Summary
Executive Summary

Connecting field evidence to the right machine configuration

Machine-specific investigations can require teams to reconcile model, options, parts, software or subsystem versions, service records and engineering history. The opportunity is to retrieve the right context for one machine without manually stitching together disconnected evidence.

The constraint to validate is whether relevant data can be connected: configuration, parts and options, subsystem or software versions, field and service signals, issue records, and approved engineering content. Access, identifiers and permissions determine what a prototype can resolve.

A potential John Deere exploration would bring the right machine configuration, service history, issue records and engineering content into a single workflow. An engineer could ask a question in plain language, review source-backed evidence and inspect the relevant component without switching between systems.

A separate, anonymized automaker reference engagement reported a reduction from 200+ minutes to under 40. That result is not a John Deere result or forecast. For John Deere, this is an illustrative starting point: resolve a field signal to the applicable machine configuration and component, then return evidence for review.

Why investigations stall

  • Evidence lives in systems that were never designed to talk to each other.
  • One issue may cross machine configuration, service, test and engineering context.
  • Every handoff adds queue time, lost context and rework.

What good looks like

  • One plain-language question over the approved evidence available to that user.
  • The applicable component resolved and shown for human confirmation.
  • Source-backed context and metadata returned to support the next decision.
The opportunity to validate

Fragmented investigations may delay answers and consume specialist time. A John Deere pilot should establish the current workflow, data sources and baseline before estimating operational or financial impact.

04 · The Solution
The Solution

A machine-context agent: evidence and component view in one workflow

The concept is an agent that could work across approved service, test and engineering sources and return evidence with the relevant machine component. Connector feasibility and authorized model access must be validated.

The differentiator

The concept combines permission-aware reasoning across enterprise context with an illustrative 3D component view. Feasibility depends on source access, connector design, configuration identifiers and the ability to expose approved engineering models.

◈

Connected, governed data

A scoped implementation would connect selected sources—such as service records, machine configuration, issue history and engineering content—while honoring source permissions. Any CAD / PLM or supplier-restricted data requires explicit feasibility and access review.

✳

Agentic orchestration

A prototype could route questions across sources selected during discovery, retrieve relevant evidence and explain how the answer was assembled, subject to permissions and evaluation.

⬡

In-workflow 3D visualization

An approved model could open inline in the workflow, ready to orbit, zoom, isolate, explode and inspect its metadata. A next step could be drafted for human review; any write-back action would require an approved workflow.
