What Glean is

Glean is the open, horizontal Work AI platform: a permission-aware Assistant, an agent platform for every department, and enterprise search — all sitting on Glean Enterprise Context, a knowledge graph that maps 60+ signals across your content, people and activity. Unlike ecosystem-bound tools, Glean connects everything your teams use, routes across 35+ models with no lock-in, and governs it all with Glean Protect. For a regulated enterprise like KeyBank, it's the secure context and control layer beneath whatever model or app comes next.

How the alternatives compare iHow each approach is positioned for enterprise-wide, cross-system AI.
  • Microsoft Copilot. Strongest inside Microsoft 365, but ecosystem-bound — limited native reach into the many systems outside M365, and models limited to Azure OpenAI / Anthropic.
  • Google Gemini. Strong models, but oriented to Google's stack — non-native sources often need to be replicated into BigQuery, and you're steered toward Gemini models.
  • Single-model tools (e.g. Claude). One excellent model, but you're tied to a single provider as the frontier keeps shifting.
  • Open-source models alone. Increasingly capable and cost-effective, but need a platform to add context, connectors, routing and governance.
  • Glean. One open, governed platform that unifies context across all of the above.

The pattern we see across enterprises
The AI market will not converge on a single model, vendor, or app. It fragments — different models for different tasks (Claude for coding, Gemini for multimodal, GPT for reasoning, open source where cost or compliance requires), different apps for different workflows, and different agents for different jobs. Betting the enterprise on one provider is betting against how the market actually evolves.
Why Glean
A horizontal platform sees interactions across every tool, so it collects the most context, routes to any model, and governs it centrally. Glean is the durable context and control layer beneath whatever model or app comes next — and it even makes Copilot and Gemini better by feeding them cross-system context. In a market that reinvents itself every six months, flexibility and governance beat lock-in.
