01 · Why Glean

The problem wasn't a lack of AI. It was too many systems.

Medtronic's sellers, specialists and operations teams work across many systems and data domains. Leadership wanted one layer that pulls context together and gets work done, without asking reps to stitch every source together by hand.

Fragmentation

One harmonizing layer

Glean connects Salesforce, Microsoft 365, Snowflake, ServiceNow and approved product content so one question returns grounded context from all of them.

Seller capacity

More time selling, not searching

Medtronic was clear that Glean doesn't replace reps. It takes on admin and self-service desk work so reps can cover more territory, convert more business and help more patients.

Speed

Faster than CRM customization

Teams can test and tailor workflows in hours, not sprints, so the business shapes the tools instead of waiting in an IT queue.

Trust & openness

Permissions, security, no data lock-in

Answers respect each person's existing access. Glean works across multiple AI front doors and third-party agents, and Medtronic keeps ownership of its data.

45 min vs. 10+ days

An equivalent agent took about 45 minutes to build in Glean, compared with more than 10 days in their CRM platform. That proof point gave Medtronic a much faster way to test and tailor seller workflows.

05 · What's next

Orion: a single workspace for the seller's whole day

With the core workflows live, Medtronic and Glean's forward deployed engineering team are prototyping a seller command center. It doesn't recreate a complex analytics dashboard. It's built around the rep's day and turns insight into action, on mobile and by voice.

01

Commercial insights from Medtronic's existing Snowflake Cortex agents, now in the flow of the rep's day.

02

Glean agents gather context, recommend next steps and take action, including creating Salesforce records by text.

03

Account pages written by agents for each account and each seller, drawing on everything Glean connects to.

04

Mobile and voice for reps moving between hospitals: dictate post-visit notes or ask about a deal while on the move.

05

A phased rollout that starts with enterprise accounts, where data is most ready, then expands.
