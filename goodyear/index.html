Microsoft Copilot Premier Suite

Productivity inside M365

Excellent for individual productivity within the Microsoft suite, but bounded to the O365 ecosystem, dependent on OpenAI models, and reliant on third-party connectors — often without item-level permissions — for everything outside it. Glean is the horizontal layer that reaches the rest of Goodyear's stack, including your ERP, PLM, and MES data.

Palantir Foundry

Complementary, not competitive

The factory for structured, high-value analytics — supply-chain optimization, quality and defect models — built for data scientists. It is complementary: Glean indexes Foundry's outputs so every employee can discover them in context, amplifying that investment rather than replacing it.

Claude (Anthropic)

Agentic assistants

Strong Claude-powered agents for knowledge workers, but agent-first rather than a shared enterprise index — a maturing connector set and a single-model family. Glean grounds any agent (including Claude or your LangChain stack) in one governed knowledge base.

The Journey · Five Phases

From foundation to the front line

Each phase stands on its own value while laying the groundwork for the next — no rip-and-replace, no waiting for the whole vision to prove one step.

01
Phase 01 · Foundation

Connect the enterprise data layer

What if Glean sat on top of Goodyear's core systems and unified enterprise data — with every existing access control carried through, untouched?
  • Glean indexes and connects to ERP, PLM, MES and surrounding systems, respecting source-level permissions on every result.
  • A single governed retrieval layer — the plumbing beneath your Microsoft and LangChain agents, not a competing platform.
  • Foundation for context, connectivity, and APIs before any commercial scale-up.
02
Phase 02 · Enablement

Engineering & IT enterprise intelligence

What if your engineers and IT teams had an enterprise intelligence layer that answered from code, tickets, docs and runbooks — in one place?
  • Developer productivity: search across repos, wikis, and knowledge without context-switching.
  • ITSM & operational efficiency — resolve issues faster with grounded, cited answers.
  • A low-risk internal launchpad that proves value and trust before customer-facing use.
03
Phase 03 · Frontline Productivity

Engineering, plant & dealer productivity

What if a plant engineer or dealer rep could ask one question and instantly pull the right spec, product detail, and account context — fully grounded, fully cited?
  • Engineers, quality teams, and dealers spend less time hunting across systems and more time on the work that matters.
  • Answers assembled from approved, permission-aware sources — no guessing, no exposure.
  • Delivered inside the tools teams already use, from Teams to plant-floor and dealer portals.
04
Phase 04 · Trust & Control

Regulatory, quality & governance

What if AI adoption strengthened your quality and compliance posture instead of complicating it — every answer traceable, auditable, and in-policy?
  • Enterprise-grade governance: access controls, audit trails, and data residency honored end-to-end.
  • Citations on every response give reviewers a clear line back to the source of record — spec, SOP, or standard.
  • Governed guardrails let regulated and safety-critical workflows adopt AI with confidence, not caveats.
05
Phase 05 · The Vision

The knowledge layer behind the dealer & fleet experience

What if Glean became the knowledge layer between your dealer, fleet, and connected-mobility experiences and the enterprise — the trusted brain your customer experience quietly runs on?
  • Every customer-facing surface draws grounded, governed answers from one intelligence layer.
  • A consistent source of truth spanning core systems, engineering, operations, and compliance.
  • Goodyear's differentiated customer experience, powered by enterprise-wide knowledge.
