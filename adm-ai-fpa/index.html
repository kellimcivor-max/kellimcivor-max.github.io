04 · The Solution

Three foundations, one governed platform.

Glean delivers exactly what Finance needs — connected context, orchestration without lock-in, and enterprise-grade governance — through three integrated pillars.

01

Enterprise Context

The Enterprise Graph + Personal Graph build a permission-aware understanding of ADM's people, content, and systems — and how finance and business teams actually work. That grounding makes retrieval precise and answers trustworthy.

02

Glean Intelligence

Intelligent routing across ~30 models, a no-code agent builder, and continual learning. Retrieval runs against Glean's index before inference — cutting AI consumption cost 25–30% while improving answer quality.

Glean Protect

AI-powered access controls, native permission mirroring, full auditability, and agent guardrails. Every retrieval respects each user's identity — so Glean never surfaces what a user isn't authorized to see.

09 · Addressing Your Concerns

We've heard the questions. Here's how we answer them.

"We're already building AI tools in-house — why buy?"

A custom build solves one team's problem but carries ongoing maintenance and technical complexity, and its knowledge stays trapped. Glean turns that proprietary corpus into a governed enterprise asset — maintained as a product — so your team builds differentiated finance agents instead of re-engineering foundation.

"We don't want to duplicate our Microsoft Copilot investment."

You won't. Glean sits underneath as the shared context layer and reduces AI consumption cost ~25–30% by serving enterprise context before external LLM calls. Copilot stays your M365 productivity surface; Glean makes retrieval enterprise-wide across every system Copilot can't reach — including SAP, planning, and BI.

"Will security and permissions hold across sensitive financial data?"

Permission-aware by default: every retrieval respects each user's identity and entitlements, with native permission mirroring, full auditability, and ADM-controlled encryption keys (CMEK/BYOK) on GCP. Glean never surfaces what a user isn't authorized to see — critical for material, pre-release, and restricted financial information.

"How fast will teams actually adopt it?"

Glean typically reaches 90%+ adoption with ~98% answer accuracy, and employees build their own agents in natural language — a direct enabler of the planning, analysis, and reporting goals finance is driving. Connector maintenance shifts off your team, managed by Glean across 250+ sources.
