02 · Executive Summary
Executive Summary

Cutting a complex dealer-to-engineering investigation from hours to minutes

Dealer adjustment and warranty investigations are among the most expensive hidden workflows in tire manufacturing. Every day of delayed root-cause analysis compounds adjustment cost, repeat returns, scrap and exposure to larger quality actions.

The constraint is that the evidence is fragmented. It is scattered across dealer claims and technician notes, retail service records, quality systems, compound research, spec and change history, Teamcenter / PLM and plant data. Engineers lose hours stitching context together before they can even identify the right component, inspect the design and decide what to do.

At a top global automaker, Glean's embedded Forward Deployed Engineering team rebuilt this investigation as a single workflow. An engineer asks a question in plain language, and the answer comes back with the right part identified and shown in 3D, ready to inspect, without leaving the screen.

The result: investigation time fell from 200+ minutes to under 40, an 82% reduction the customer independently valued at $100M+ per year in recovered engineering capacity alone. For Goodyear, the same pattern starts with a dealer signal and ends with the affected tire component, its spec and its change history in one view.

Why investigations stall

  • Evidence lives in systems that were never designed to talk to each other.
  • Each call spans compound, construction, supply chain and plant, with downstream impact on every tire built.
  • Every handoff adds queue time, lost context and rework.

What good looks like

  • One question, in plain language, asked against all the evidence at once.
  • The right component identified and rendered for visual confirmation.
  • Change history and metadata attached, ready to decide and act.
A structural, company-wide cost

Slow investigations directly increase adjustment and warranty spend, delay containment and extend issue cycles. They also pull the most expensive technical talent away from prevention and product improvement. At enterprise scale, this friction becomes one of the largest hidden cost centers in engineering.

04 · The Solution
The Solution

A 360° engineering agent: reasoning and visualization in one surface

This is not another search box or a standalone CAD viewer. It is an agent that works across engineering and dealer-network systems and brings the answer, including the physical component itself, into the conversation.

The differentiator

Other approaches force a choice: a generic chatbot that can reason but can't show the part, or a CAD viewer that can show the part but can't reason. Glean delivers governed enterprise reasoning and interactive 3D visualization in one workflow, so engineers can work with complex data and CAD designs in real time.

◈

Connected, governed data

Glean reasons across connected systems of record (dealer claims, quality, engineering, change history and component metadata) while honoring every existing permission. CAD and PLM hold the most sensitive IP, supplier-restricted data and prerelease designs, so each user sees only what they are entitled to.

✳

Agentic orchestration

The agent breaks each question down, pulls from the right systems across 12+ connected sources and builds the answer step by step. It guides the engineer through the evidence while respecting every access rule.

⬡

In-workflow 3D visualization

The relevant model opens live in the chat, ready to orbit, zoom, isolate, explode and inspect its metadata. The engineer can then act on the findings, for example by creating a change-request ticket.
