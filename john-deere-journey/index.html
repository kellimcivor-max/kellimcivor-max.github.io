Microsoft Copilot (M365)

Productivity inside M365

Excellent for individual productivity within the Microsoft suite, but bounded to the O365 ecosystem, dependent on OpenAI models, and reliant on third-party connectors — often without item-level permissions — for everything outside it. Glean is the horizontal layer that reaches the rest of Deere's stack, including Teamcenter/PLM, SAP, Salesforce, code repositories, and the engineering systems where machines are designed.

GitHub Copilot

Engineering IDEs

Glean grounds engineers in the full history and downstream impact of a change across every product-development system, and can feed that governed context back into the IDE.

The Journey · Five Phases

From the drafting table to the dealer lot

Each phase stands on its own value while laying the groundwork for the next — no rip-and-replace, no waiting for the whole vision to prove one step. This is the plan that starts where Deere builds machines and grows to the enterprise.

01
Phase 01 · Foundation

Connect the enterprise data layer

What if Glean sat on top of Deere's core systems and unified engineering knowledge — firewalled, with every existing access control carried through, untouched?
  • Glean indexes and connects Teamcenter/PLM, code repositories, JIRA, SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow, and the schematics and requirements docs that define each machine, respecting source-level permissions on every result.
  • A single governed retrieval layer that sits inside your enterprise — nothing goes out, nothing comes in — beneath your Copilot and in-house AI ecosystem, not a competing platform.
  • Connectors and evaluation shipped as a product: no manual metadata layers, no per-source engineering program to maintain.
02
Phase 02 · The Wedge

Product development & engineering intelligence (ISG / Ag & Turf)

What if an engineer could act on a change immediately — design intent, risk assessments, schematics, requirements, code, and tickets surfaced in one place — instead of reconstructing history from five or six APIs and four or five data hubs first?
  • Establish a durable, agentic product-development workflow across the personas who build embedded, electrical, and hardware systems — development and software engineers, electrical engineers, product managers, and customer-support engineers.
  • Glean surfaces relevant datasets, prior AI model performance logs, and experiment results, so AI/ML teams spend less time gathering information and more time tuning models for autonomy, fleet optimization, and predictive maintenance.
  • A high-value, contained wedge in a product org that cares about outcomes over infrastructure — proving trust before broader rollout.
03
Phase 03 · Frontline Productivity

Dealer network, customer support & service

What if a support engineer or dealer technician could ask one question and instantly pull the right part, service bulletin, and machine configuration — fully grounded, fully cited?
  • Support and dealer teams spend less time hunting across systems and more time keeping machines running, with answers assembled from approved, permission-aware sources.
  • Every response respects who the user is — surfacing only the documentation, diagnostics, and records a given role or dealer is entitled to see.
  • Delivered inside the tools teams already use, from Teams to service and dealer-support consoles.
04
Phase 04 · Trust & Control

Quality, safety, security & IP protection

What if AI adoption strengthened Deere's security and IP posture instead of complicating it — every answer traceable, auditable, and in-policy?
  • Enterprise-grade governance: access controls, audit trails, and data residency honored end-to-end — designed to protect proprietary designs and engineering IP.
  • Citations on every response give reviewers a clear line back to the source of record — a spec, schematic, quality record, or safety procedure.
  • Governed guardrails let regulated, safety-critical, and model-risk workflows adopt AI with confidence, aligned to Deere's own AI governance standards.
05
Phase 05 · The Vision

The knowledge layer behind the connected machine

What if Glean became the knowledge layer between Deere's people, products, and dealers and the enterprise — the trusted brain your teams quietly run on?
  • Every engineer-, dealer-, and customer-facing surface draws grounded, governed answers from one intelligence layer.
  • A consistent source of truth spanning product development, manufacturing, supply base, and the dealer network.
  • Deere's build-to-last reputation, powered by enterprise-wide knowledge at everyone's fingertips.
